# Producer review

Reviewed in the repository's declared role order; these are producer checks,
not independent role signoffs.

- Simulation Auditor: comparisons delegate to the native core; golden responses
  cover outcome directions and invalid records. No experiment is simulated by
  the page and no causal/adoption inference is made.
- Evidence Custodian and Adversarial Evidence Reviewer: original uploaded text
  and unknown-field warnings are separate from labelled known-field views.
  Source custody and native/WASM responses are inspectable; caller provenance
  remains an assertion.
- Runtime Boundary Engineer: no changes to foundation dependencies, features,
  API or lockfile. Browser-only dependencies live in a standalone unpublished
  workspace. File/worker revisions fence stale asynchronous completions.
- Decision Skeptic: improved metrics do not erase error findings. No new
  adoption, deletion or external-consumer claim is attached to this UI.
- Consumer Advocate: two editable records, six synthetic examples, actionable
  diagnostics, original/comparison downloads and source access support a real
  comparison task without a local Rust installation.

Qualification results are recorded in WAVE.md after the actual checks run.
